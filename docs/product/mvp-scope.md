# MVP Scope

## Promise

Upload invoices, bills, receipts, and bank statements. LedgerAI India creates a clean accounting
workspace, prepares GST-ready records, drafts invoices and return summaries, compares tax options,
and tells users what needs review.

## Primary Users

- Freelancers and consultants.
- Small service businesses.
- Small trading/manufacturing organizations.
- Accountants and CAs serving client workspaces.

## MVP Modules

1. Workspace dashboard with compliance health, review feed, GST period status, sales, expenses,
   ITC estimate, receivables, due dates, and AI actions awaiting approval.
2. India business onboarding with PAN, GSTINs, states, business type, filing frequency, turnover,
   income-tax profile, and accountant invite.
3. Document inbox for PDFs/images/CSV/Excel/GST exports with private storage, classification,
   extraction, validation, review, approval, and posting.
4. GST invoice builder with seller/buyer GSTINs, place of supply, HSN/SAC, CGST/SGST/IGST,
   export flags, PDF/email placeholders, and status lifecycle.
5. GST cockpit for GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B drafts, validation cards, source drill-down, JSON/Excel
   placeholders, external filing marker, and period lock.
6. Freelancer tax cockpit for 44AD/44ADA eligibility, old/new regime comparison, advance-tax
   reminders, and CA export pack.
7. AI junior accountant assistant that cites internal records and requires approval for mutation.

## Non-Goals For MVP

- Direct GST filing.
- Live e-invoice/e-waybill generation.
- Account Aggregator bank feeds.
- UPI Autopay.
- Official ITR filing.

These are planned integrations and must remain explicit adapter flows with human approval.
